The purpose of this notification is to inform you of the status of your room/resource request. If the notification indicates:
  • Accepted - you do not need to do anything further.
  • Tentative - please checkto see if the room/resource is available at the requested time. If it is not available at the requested time, please reschedule by selecting a new time or by removing the original room/resource and selecting a new room/resource.
  • Denied - room is not available please find a new room. Hint: use the Room Finder feature to find an available room.
Standard Room Booking Policy is:
  • Not more than 90 days into the future
  • No meeting is longer than 24 hours in length - Hint: use the Reoccurence feature rather than booking a meeting that spans multiple days.

If your request is outside of this policy the Room/Resource Delegates for this room will review and Accept or Decline your request.
