The purpose of this notification is to inform you of the status of your room/resource request. If the notification indicates:
  • Accepted - you do not need to do anything further
  • Denied - room is not available or you are trying to book a repeating meeting which are not allowed. Hint: use the Room Finder feature to find an available room.
Hoteling Room Booking Policy is:
  • Not more than 90 days into the future
  • No Recurring, Repeating or Multiday Meetings Allowed - Hint: Recurrences can be removed from a meeting by editing the meeting on the Recurrence tab select "Remove Recurrence".
  • No meeting is longer than 24 hours in length.

If your request is outside of this policy then review your request and resubmit so it is within the policy.
